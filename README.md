## 🎮 About the Game

Inferno Protocol is a first-person co-op survival horror game developed and published by Tektonic Interactive. It entered Steam Early Access on August 28, 2026. Players explore a vast, constantly changing maze solo or with up to six players, scavenging resources, crafting equipment, fighting hostile creatures, and searching for a way out.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Inferno Protocol**. This trainer provides optional assistance with survival, exploration, combat, crafting, and inventory management in Inferno Protocol. It is designed around the game's resource gathering, weapon use, creature harvesting, traps, settlements, and loader-based abilities.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed solo experience, faster crafting and experimentation, easier maze exploration, or additional support while learning the game's survival, combat, and loader systems.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents the player from losing health during exploration and hostile encounters.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Removes stamina limits for extended movement and evasive actions.
- **Unlimited loader Uses** — Prevents supported loaders from being consumed when activating abilities such as dash, rage, night vision, double jump, and extra health.
- **Extended Ability Duration** — Extends the active duration of supported loader-based abilities.
- **Safe Exploration Mode** — Combines health, stamina, and resource assistance for players who want to focus on the maze, settlements, and environmental discovery.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Infinite Ammunition** — Provides unlimited ammunition for supported firearms and ranged weapons.
- **No Reload** — Allows supported weapons to fire without manual reloading.
- **Trap Supply** — Provides a continuous supply of supported deployable traps for defensive setups and corridor ambushes.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Crafting Materials** — Keeps usable quantities of gathered resources available for crafting gear, weapons, and traps.
- **Item Quantity Editor** — Allows supported inventory quantities to be adjusted for resources, harvested creature parts, and consumable items.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game and load into a session before activating movement-related assistance. Unlimited stamina is useful for long journeys through the maze and for avoiding creatures. loader abilities can provide additional mobility through dash and double jump, while night vision helps during darker exploration sections.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect resources, harvested creature parts, weapons, loaders, and other items normally. Open the inventory after picking up an item, then use the quantity feature on supported entries. Unlimited crafting materials are useful for producing equipment and traps without repeatedly returning to the central settlement.

### World & Environment

Inferno Protocol takes place in a massive circular labyrinth whose layout changes as players explore it. Use wall markings and environmental landmarks to maintain orientation. The central settlement supports trading, farming, construction, and crafting, while smaller camps can provide additional interactions and supplies.

### Combat & Weapons

Weapons, traps, and environmental awareness are essential when confronting creatures that respond to sound and scent. Infinite ammunition and no-reload assistance can simplify firefights, while unlimited health and stamina provide additional room for evasive movement. Harvest defeated creatures to obtain materials used in crafting.
